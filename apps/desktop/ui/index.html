Choose the actual speakers or headphones RoomSync should play through, not a virtual cable. It must differ from the capture output.

Set up the route in Windows first

  1. Send your source app or Windows playback to CABLE Input (or your virtual playback device).
  2. Turn off Listen to this device and any other mixer or monitor route for that device. Direct monitoring can cause echo or feedback.
  3. Choose that virtual device above, then your physical speakers. Start at a low listening volume.

RoomSync does not install a driver or change Windows routing. It cannot verify your external mixer or monitoring settings.

RoomSync chooses the lowest shared lead that covers measured device queues plus a safety reserve, at or above this minimum. It is not zero latency or measured speaker delay. VPN and virtual networks may not work.

Connection details

    Keep the RoomSync console open. Pairing waits until you press Stop.

    Keep the PC’s speakers inaudible to avoid echo. Headphones work.

    After Stop, Windows routing stays unchanged. Audio still sent to the virtual device may be silent. Switch your app or Windows output back to your physical speakers yourself.

    Privacy & preview notes

    Local controls. No cloud relay or microphone capture. Codes stay in memory and are shared only when you reveal them. Stop and restart with the same code, or create a new one to replace it.

    Use a trusted local network. Never expose the audio ports to the internet. RoomSync doesn’t change your firewall or mute your PC. This preview still needs physical-device synchronization testing.
